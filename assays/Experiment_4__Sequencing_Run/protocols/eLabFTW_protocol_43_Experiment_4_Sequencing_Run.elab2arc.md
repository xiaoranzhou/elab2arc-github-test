Illumina NovaSeq 6000 sequencing 
=================================

Goal:
=====

Sequencing of samples from [Assay - Experiment 3: Library Preparation - FFPE Repair, A-tailing, Adapter Ligation](https://elab.dataplan.top/experiments.php?mode=view&id=20)

Methods:
========

*   **Sequencing Platform:** Illumina NovaSeq 6000
*   **Flow Cell:** SP Flow Cell (Lot No. NV-FC-20250622A)
*   **Chemistry:** NovaSeq 6000 S1 Reagent Kit v1.5 (300 cycles)
*   **Loading Concentration:** 200 pM
*   **Read Length:** 2x150 bp paired-end reads

.

Results:
========

*   **Yield:** Approximately 10-15 Gb per sample.
*   **Q30 Score:** >90% across all samples.
*   **Data Location:** FASTQ files are stored on the institutional `smb://institutionserver/microbial_genomics_data/raw_reads/`
